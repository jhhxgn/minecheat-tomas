How to Start

Step 1: Turn on cheats

1Make a new test world.
2Open the world settings.
3Turn on Activate Cheats.
Old world? Tap Pause, then Settings, then Game, then Activate Cheats.

Step 2: Open the chat

1Play your world.
2Tap the chat bubble at the top.
3Type / and look for the list.
The list shows? Cheats work!

Step 3: Use a command

1Tap Copy on a card.
2Open the chat in the game.
3Hold the text box. Tap Paste.
4Tap Send.
